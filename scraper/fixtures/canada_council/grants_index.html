Grants

The Canada Council for the Arts helps individual artists, arts workers, groups and arts organizations engage in projects and activities. 

In 2024–25, more than 3,000 Canadian artists, 390 groups and 1,950 arts organizations received Canada Council grants.

Apply nowLearn more

Find funding for your needs

Our grants are designed to support all types of applicants. Explore relevant funding opportunities on the client portal.

For individuals

Artists and arts workers developing their work or advancing their careers can apply for funding. Artists, curators, critics and other arts workers seeking support for artistic projects, professional development or distribution and sharing are included.

For groups

Artist collectives, ensembles and creative partnerships working together on artistic projects or supporting and promoting Canadian artists, arts communities or artistic practices can apply for funding. Groups of collaborating artists who share artistic goals but don’t have formal organizational structures are included.

For organizations

Established arts entities with formal governance structures, including theatre companies, galleries, festivals and other arts organizations or service organizations can apply for funding.

The Canada Council’s grant programs

Grants are organized under programs to deliver outcome-based funding with a measurable impact on the arts and culture in Canada.

The Canada Council for the Arts updated its grants programming in 2025. Grants are now organized under 5 programs, each with several funding opportunities:

Explore and Create

Supports the research, development, creation and production of artistic work.

Learn more about this program

Engage and Sustain

Supports arts organizations at the heart of creative communities across Canada.

Learn more about this program

Creating, Knowing and Sharing: The Arts and Cultures of First Nations, Inuit and Métis Peoples

Supports individuals, groups, Indigenous-led arts organizations and arts and cultural sector development organizations that foster a vital and resilient Indigenous arts ecosystem.

Learn more about this program

Supporting Artistic Practice

Supports Canadian arts professionals, groups and arts organizations who champion the Canadian arts sector, boost the capacity for artists to realize work and advance the conditions of creation.

Learn more about this program

Arts Across Canada and Abroad

Supports Canadian artists in sharing their work both nationally and internationally.

Learn more about this program

In addition to Canada Council's grant programs, applicants may be eligible for other funding designed to address specific opportunities or challenges within the arts sector.

Get help with your application

You may be eligible for Application Assistance to pay someone to help you apply for a grant if you have difficulty with the application process and self-identify as:

• an artist who is Deaf, hard of hearing, has a disability or is living with a mental illness; or

• a First Nations, Inuit or Métis artist facing language, geographic and/or cultural barriers.
